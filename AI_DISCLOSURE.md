# AI and reuse disclosure

Breakwater is a new ETHOnline 2026 Classic project. No code, UI, design asset, or
other implementation from an earlier Qdee project is reused.

OpenAI Codex is being used after kickoff for research assistance, implementation,
testing, review, and documentation. All generated or suggested work is subject
to maintainer review and the repository's automated tests.

The project begins from 1inch's official `swap-vm-template` and imports official
Aqua and SwapVM dependencies. Their source commits, licenses, and notices are
preserved and recorded in `DEPENDENCIES.md`, `LICENSE`, `LICENSES/`, and
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` once the upstream baseline is imported.
